Register · Create your wallet

We use Trust Wallet — a free, self-custodial app. You hold your own money, no bank needed. Setup takes about 5 minutes.

1

Download the official app

Install “Trust Wallet” from Google Play or the App Store, or from trustwallet.com. Check the developer name — fake wallet apps exist.

2

Create a new wallet

Open the app and choose “Create a New Wallet” (use “Import” only if you are restoring an old wallet). Accept the terms.

3

Set a PIN & biometric lock

Set a PIN code, then enable fingerprint / Face ID if you like. The PIN lives only on your phone — if you forget it, restore the wallet with your 12 words.

4

Open the backup menu

Tap the wallet name at the top of the home screen → on the Wallets screen choose “Back up manually” → enter your PIN to reveal the phrase.

5

Write down your 12 words

Your 12-word secret recovery phrase appears. Write all 12 words on paper in the exact order, then confirm by re-entering them. Keep the paper safe and offline.

6

Your wallet is ready

Done! You can now receive, send and hold crypto. Next, open “Get QR & address” to enable USDT and find your receiving address.

⚠ Your 12 words = your money

Anyone with these words can take everything in your wallet. Never share them with anyone — not even support. Never type them into a website or take a screenshot. No legitimate service will ever ask for them.

Get your QR code & USDT address

1

On the home screen tap the filter / toggle icon → “Manage crypto” → search “USDT” → turn on the one labelled “BNB Smart Chain” (BEP20).

2

Open USDT (BNB Smart Chain)

Back on the home screen, tap the USDT entry that shows “BNB Smart Chain”.

3

Tap Receive — see the QR & address

Tap “Receive”. A QR code and your address (starting with 0x) appear, with the network shown as BNB Smart Chain.

4

Copy the address or share the QR

Tap “Copy” to copy the address, or “Share” to send the QR. The address is like a bank account number — safe to share. The QR lets the sender scan instead of typing.

5

Send it to GeGe Team

Withdraw · Turn USDT into cash

Send USDT to an exchange, then sell it via P2P to your bank or e-wallet (IDR / PHP).

1

Create an exchange account

Register on Binance, OKX or Bybit and complete identity verification (KYC) with your ID. This is required before you can sell.

2

Get your USDT deposit address

In the exchange: Deposit → USDT → network “BNB Smart Chain (BEP20)” → copy the deposit address. The network must match or funds can be lost.

3

Send USDT from Trust Wallet

Trust Wallet → USDT (BEP20) → Send → paste the exchange deposit address → enter the amount → confirm. You need a little BNB (usually under $0.10) for the network fee. Tip: send a small test amount first.

4

Sell via P2P and get cash

In the exchange open P2P Trading → Sell → USDT → choose your currency (IDR / PHP) and payment method. Pick a buyer with many orders and a high completion rate. Release the USDT only after the money has actually arrived in your bank / e-wallet.

🇮🇩Indonesia: bank transfer (BCA, Mandiri, BRI…), DANA, OVO, GoPay
🇵🇭Philippines: GCash, Maya, bank transfer (BDO, BPI, UnionBank…)

⚠ P2P safety

Never release your crypto before the cash is visible in your own bank / e-wallet app. Ignore payment screenshots from the buyer — check your real balance.

Deposit · Put funds into your wallet

Top up your wallet with USDT (to hold) or a little BNB (to pay sending fees). Receiving is always free.

1

From an exchange

In your exchange: Withdraw → USDT (or BNB) → network “BNB Smart Chain (BEP20)” → paste YOUR wallet address from “Get QR & address” → confirm. Funds arrive in 1–5 minutes.

2

Buy inside the app

Trust Wallet → Buy → choose USDT or BNB → pay by card or local method where available. The crypto lands straight in your wallet.

3

From another wallet

In the other wallet: Send → paste your address → select the BNB Smart Chain (BEP20) network → confirm.

4

Receive payment from GeGe Team

Send your address to the admin on Discord (see “Get QR & address”). After the in-game trade we send the agreed USDT — it arrives in 1–5 minutes.

Safety rules — read before sending money

  • Never share your 12-word phrase or private key. No legitimate support will ever ask for it.
  • Both sides must use the same network: BNB Smart Chain (BEP20).
  • Check the address carefully — first and last 6 characters. Clipboard malware can swap addresses.

Frequently asked questions

What is BEP20?

BEP20 is the token standard of BNB Smart Chain (BSC), the blockchain built by Binance. USDT on BEP20 transfers in minutes and costs only a few cents, which is why we use it for payments.

Why do I need BNB if I get paid in USDT?

Every transaction on BNB Smart Chain pays its network fee in BNB. Keeping about $1 of BNB covers many USDT transfers. Receiving USDT is free — you only need BNB when you send.

What if I sent USDT on the wrong network?

Contact the receiving platform's support immediately with the transaction ID (TXID). Recovery is sometimes possible if both networks share the same address format, but it is not guaranteed — always double-check the network before sending.

How long does a transfer take?

Wallet ↔ exchange: usually 1–5 minutes. P2P sale: depends on the buyer, often 5–30 minutes. Bank / e-wallet transfers are usually instant.

Is there a minimum amount?

There is no minimum to receive USDT in your wallet. Exchanges and P2P buyers set their own minimums, usually a few dollars.

I lost my phone. Is my money gone?

No — if you kept your 12-word phrase. Install Trust Wallet on a new phone, choose “Import wallet” and enter the 12 words. Your USDT will be there. If you lost both the phrase and the phone, the funds cannot be recovered.
